Pico W 1 (LED)
- GPIO 14 -> rezistor 220-1000 ohmu -> anoda LED (delsi nozicka)
- katoda LED (kratsi nozicka) -> GND

Pico W 2 (tlacitko)
- jedna strana tlacitka -> GPIO 13
- druha strana tlacitka -> GND
- program pouziva interni pull-up rezistor, externi neni potreba

Mezi deskami nepropojuj GPIO ani GND. Komunikace probiha bezdratove pres Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
